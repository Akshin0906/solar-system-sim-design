# AI assistance and technical ownership

`Akshin0906` is the project's primary maintainer and author and remains
accountable for its product decisions, architecture, scientific framing,
security, and released behavior.

AI coding assistants have been used for scoped implementation, review,
documentation, and verification work. When an assistant made a material
contribution to a commit, the public history retains a truthful co-author trailer
instead of presenting that contribution as unassisted work.

Assistant output is treated as a proposed change, not as authority. It is
reviewed against the repository's source contracts, primary scientific
references, deterministic verifiers, static checks, and browser tests as
appropriate. The maintainer is responsible for accepting, revising, or rejecting
the result and for correcting defects that pass through review.

AI attribution is not a substitute for technical ownership. The maintainer
should be able to explain, modify, test, and defend the code and its tradeoffs
without relying on generated prose or hidden context.
